WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

The quiet brief counts, too.

Qloo discovers books from your selected cultural references. Counterpoint collects a shared pool of up to 24 books, then asks Qloo to rank that same pool for each brief.

For each possible slate, a brief’s coverage is its strongest book score. We maximize the lowest coverage first, then the next lowest, and so on. This is called leximin. The total-first comparison instead maximizes the sum of those coverage scores.

Both selections are exact within this small candidate pool. A different candidate pool or scoring rule can change the answer. Cross-domain associations help discovery; they cannot tell you what an individual will enjoy.

A hash detects receipt edits. A separate enumerator verifies the result. Neither is a Qloo signature or evidence of sales impact.
